About HelloExam

HelloExam (www.helloexam.in), developed by Firefly Labz, is a modern competitive exam preparation platform built on three core pillars: Practice • Improve • Achieve.

Designed for students and competitive exam aspirants, HelloExam simplifies exam readiness by combining structured practice quizzes, real-time performance analytics, and healthy peer competition in an intuitive, distraction-free dashboard.

Data Security, Third-Party Services & Your Rights

Encryption & Safeguards: All network communication between the HelloExam app/website and our cloud servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS / TLS 1.2+. User credentials and sensitive tokens are encrypted at rest and protected using modern access controls.
